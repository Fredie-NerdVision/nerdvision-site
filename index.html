FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Do I really own everything?

Yes. The domain, hosting account and WordPress install are registered in your name with your card. We get a login from you — not the other way round. When the project ends, remove our user and everything keeps working.

What is ACF and why should I care?

Advanced Custom Fields turns each editable part of your site into a labelled box in your dashboard: “Headline”, “Opening hours”, “Hero photo”. You change the text, hit Update, and the design stays intact. No page builders, no accidental layout breakage.

Can you refresh my existing site instead of starting over?

Usually — and it's often the smarter choice. We keep your domain, content and search rankings, rebuild the front end, and redirect every old link. A modern site without starting from zero.

What happens after launch?

You get a recorded walkthrough, a cheat sheet, and an hour of hands-on training. After that most clients never need us — but we're a message away if you want new features.

Is there a monthly fee?

Not to us. You pay your own hosting and domain (typically $10–25 a month, to the provider, in your name). Optional care plans exist if you'd rather we handle updates.

How long does it take?

Launchpad sites go live in two to three weeks. Accelerator and Supernova builds typically take four to eight weeks depending on integrations.

Why not just use Wix, Squarespace or an AI site builder?

They're fine for a weekend project. For a business they cost more over time, load slowly because of the scripts they bundle, look like every other site on the same template, and can't be moved — stop paying and it's gone. A NerdVision build is fast, yours outright, and portable to any developer. If you're on one now, we migrate it with your rankings intact.
